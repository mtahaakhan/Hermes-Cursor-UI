# Community code of conduct

We want participation in Hermes Cursor UI to be safe, constructive, and
welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about impact.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's credentials, logs, or
  personal information.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused and reproducible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, and deliberate publication of private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The
maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
